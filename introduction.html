Introduction

October 20, 2026

I wanted a small place on the internet where I could share my thoughts.

I don't have everything figured out. Writing might help me understand what I think, and why.

Many people say life is short, but I think that's something we say when we look back on it. Living through it feels long. It's the longest experience any of us will ever have.

As humans, we're always trying to understand what life means. I don't have a definite answer. For now, I want to preserve something of myself here—to leave a trace of who I am.

I'll share some of my thoughts here, along with things I learn and interesting things I find on the internet. I hope you find something interesting or useful.
